Questions

Before you ask.

What is Quantum Optics Lab?

Quantum Optics Lab is a free, browser-based 3D simulator for geometric optics, polarization and quantum optics. It includes 40 optical systems plus experiments such as the double slit, Hong–Ou–Mandel interference, Bell/CHSH tests, optical cavities and Wigner functions.

Is Quantum Optics Lab free?

Yes. It is free to use and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license (CC BY 4.0).

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser, on desktop or phone.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The controls are touch-first with large tap targets, and the settings panel collapses when you touch the 3D scene so the experiment gets the full screen.

Is the physics real or illustrative?

Rays are traced with Snell’s and Fresnel relations. Quantum scenes sample the actual probability distributions.

Who is it for?

Students, teachers, photonics newcomers, and curious people who want to see light behave.
